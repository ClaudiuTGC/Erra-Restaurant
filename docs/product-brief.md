# Erra Restaurant — product brief

## Visitors and goals
Local diners, visitors to Bucharest, and guests arranging a meal with friends.
1. Read the menu and understand the food and prices.
2. Find the restaurant, contact details, and reliable opening information.
3. Request a table, understanding that the restaurant must confirm it.

## Sitemap
Romanian: `/`, `/meniu/`, `/despre/`, `/contact/`.
English: `/en/`, `/en/menu/`, `/en/about/`, `/en/contact/`.
Shared: `/404.html`, `/sitemap.xml`, `/robots.txt`.

## Content hierarchy
Home: identity and reservation action; food philosophy; three dishes from the menu; location and contact.
Menu: source/date caveat; searchable category navigation; legible dishes, descriptions, and aligned prices; call to confirm.
About: cuisine and Chef Alex Teisanu; editorial illustration awaiting real photography.
Contact: address, click-to-call, unknown-hours notice, map loaded on request, and reservation request form.

## Direction
Warm paper, ink, deep olive, and terracotta. Cormorant Garamond headings with DM Sans body. Large editorial headings, restrained rules, generous spacing. Custom botanical/table illustrations substitute for unavailable photography, never represented as photographs of the restaurant.

## Evidence and assumptions
Name, address (Strada Constantin C. Nottara 13, București), and phone (0734 983 984) come from the supplied Maps screenshot. The screenshot does not establish a weekly schedule. No hours are invented. Chef name comes from the brief. The menu screenshot is dated February 2020; prices are historical, not verified current offers. Only confidently readable names and descriptions are transcribed. English is a translation of the Romanian source, not an independent menu. No diet/allergen claims are inferred.

## Quality gates
Static build, TypeScript checks, route/link checks, keyboard and automated accessibility checks, responsive screenshots at 390/768/1440, reservation validation and error/success tests, and Lighthouse mobile measurement. No claim of real delivery without a configured and verified reservation service.
